About Hope of Living

Hope of Living is a community-focused initiative based in Lower Philippi, Cape Town. Our purpose is to encourage positive change by supporting people and strengthening communities.

Our Story

Every community has people with different needs, experiences and dreams. Hope of Living recognises the importance of coming together to create opportunities and offer support.

Through community involvement, cooperation and shared responsibility, we aim to contribute towards a stronger and more hopeful future.

Our Mission

Our mission is to promote community support, encourage participation and help create opportunities that can improve people's lives.

Our Vision

Our vision is to see stronger, more connected communities where individuals and families have opportunities to grow and thrive.

Our Values

Compassion

Treating people with kindness, care and understanding.

Community

Encouraging cooperation and supporting one another.

Respect

Valuing every individual and treating everyone with dignity.

Hope

Believing in positive change and better opportunities for the future.
